Probabilities over moves. Every chosen move is executed.

The model chooses. The game executes.

Small decisions, made by a language model.

Each turn, we give the model the board, the goal, and three directions. It scores those choices in one pass. The highest-probability move becomes the next action.

We adapted Qwen3.5-9B on 350,857 examples of instructions, reasoning and decisions. This demo uses our released supervised checkpoint while broader training continues.

What does the model see?

The game supplies coordinates, recent moves, and exact immediate collision and distance observations. It removes reverse turns. The model selects the direction; collisions end the game.
