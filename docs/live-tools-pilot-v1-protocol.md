This is a diagnostic from the original Qwen3.5-9B supervised step-2,742 adapter.

full pretraining run, production promotion, or independent release evaluation is
authorized by this protocol. The original cumulative $500 remains the limit;
current conservative unallocated balance is $192.64. A new rental needs a fresh
billing/rate reconciliation, at most $40 reserved from that balance, a provider
stop deadline and artifact recovery. No rental is allocated by writing this file.

Compare forecast supervision, reward-only Proximal Policy Optimization, and both,
with seeds 20260924 and 20260925. All six arms reload identical language weights
and zero-initialized detached critics. General replay, forecasts, world and cost
schedules match within each seed; actual actions come from each current actor.
Only real receipts provide rewards. Forecast labels remain independently executed
command-then-stop or explicitly displayed fixed continuations; they do not predict
an unspecified evolving policy. The older shell collector's unused first-option
placeholder is removed before scoring and does not become a supervised target.

The five qualified training mechanisms are configuration repair, SQLite repair,
application message delivery through ToolSandbox, filesystem scope, and reservation
transactions. Add the existing retail order/profile/payment goals through isolated
Sierra retail tools. Retail remains one connected training ownership group: three
goals, ten physical initial states, twenty goal/world combinations and 120 distinct
goal/world/fee cells. Its 144-row balanced schedule repeats the smaller payment
world set to match the two address goals. This is not 144 new tasks. Known calendar,
payment and phone evaluation results are development evidence. Reserved TOUCAN and
telecom model scores remain unopened and may not select these checkpoints.

A full arm has at most 24 single-pass optimizer updates, two live episodes per shell
mechanism and six retail episodes per update in reward arms. Forecast arms use four
questions per shell mechanism plus eight retail forecasts. Every arm uses 64 general
replay questions per update. Recipes and exact schedules are bound by the generated
freeze; partial runs report the actual prefix consumed, including any imbalance.
There is no replay of old policy trajectories and no advantage centering. Raw rewards
and costs remain intact in receipts. Shell learning units use scale one; retail raw
returns are divided by twenty exactly once. The critic already predicts those units.
Policy gradients average over observed transitions; outcome and replay losses each
average within their own component. Longer trajectories therefore contribute more
transitions, which is disclosed rather than called equal-task weighting.

Language learning rate is 0.0000002; critic rate 0.0001; forecast weight 0.25; replay
weight 1; value weight 0.5; entropy weight 0.01. Action selection mixes 80% native
probabilities with 20% uniform over the offered menu. Forecasts and replay use native
probabilities. The guard checks both native and behavior distributions, mean divergence
at most 0.02 and individual divergence at most 0.10, with transactional rollback of
weights, optimizer and random state after rejection. No automatic retry follows it.
Actual action probabilities are rescored with gradients enabled before every update;
stale actor or critic identities, missing horizons, nonfinite values and overlength
inputs abort rather than crop data. Component gradient diagnostics are backward-only
presentations and are reported separately from optimizer training.

Evaluate the already exposed report mechanism's 36 worlds and 308 forecast questions,
and the existing 622-question general retention cohort, before training and every
eight updates. Stop immediately on general macro accuracy decline exceeding one
percentage point, general log-loss increase exceeding 0.02, report return decline
exceeding 0.02, report expected Brier increase exceeding 0.02, or any rejected update.

expected Brier reduction at least 0.02, while satisfying retention. Among eligible
checkpoints maximize return minus 0.25 times expected Brier. Stop after two checks
without a new eligible improvement, earliest update sixteen. These development
criteria cannot promote a release or substitute for whole-mechanism transfer.
