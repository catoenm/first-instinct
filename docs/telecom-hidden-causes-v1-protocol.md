# Paired hidden causes and costly observations over real telecom tools

This local stage builds on the completed three-mechanism qualification. Do not
repeat its controls or modify its frozen sources. It collects new counterfactual
branches at a different public history, without models, training or rented compute.
Use the same pinned source, isolated runtime, account fixture, deterministic UUID
stream, socket block and strict exception propagation.

## Worlds, information and ownership

Enumerate four equiprobable initial configurations for each mechanism:

- Device switches: airplane mode on/off crossed with mobile data on/off. All
  other service prerequisites are healthy. Ownership: training candidates.
- Coupled roaming: carrier permission on/off crossed with device roaming on/off,
  while abroad with healthy hardware and sufficient allowance. Ownership:
  development candidates, with no training use of any related variant.
- Carrier allowance: allowance available/exhausted crossed with mobile data
  on/off. Ownership: reserved transfer candidates; no model selection or training.

The 12 configurations include the three already qualified baseline configurations
and nine new combinations. They are not 12 independent mechanisms. Keep every
cost, wording, observation and branch variant with its mechanism. Do not use the
official benchmark tasks or count this as an official tau result.
Also report exact physical-state deduplication across mechanisms; distinct task
contracts can share an underlying state and must not inflate that count.

The initial actor history contains only the public customer lookup for the task's
known phone number. Obtain subsequent identifiers from that response. It must be
byte-identical across all four configurations within a mechanism. The complaint
is historical; one configuration already works. Initializer flags, configuration
IDs and verifier state never enter actor observations or proposed options.

The public task contract identifies which two settings may be wrong, states their
independent 50/50 prior, and states that the remaining prerequisites are healthy.
That defines the finite probability exercise; it is not a claim about failure
frequencies in real telecom customers. Executions, not model votes, supply labels.

## Declared alternatives and continuation semantics

Execute six alternatives from each initial configuration, each in a fresh world,
then independently replay each alternative once: 144 world executions maximum.
Each world permits at most 16 total public-tool/verifier calls and 30 seconds;
the collection permits at most 2,304 calls. Preserve failed attempts and stop the
collector on any unexpected adapter/infrastructure error. Do not retry automatically.

The six alternatives are stopping; toggling device data; one mechanism-specific
blind action (toggle airplane mode, enable carrier roaming, or buy one gigabyte);
inspect and repair; repeat the service inspection and then repair; and attempt a
documentedly invalid zero-gigabyte top-up, observe its error, then inspect/repair.
For that last control, accept only the exact expected `ValueError` and message,
and require the failed command to preserve the complete state. Other exceptions
are infrastructure failures, never negative examples.

The fixed inspect/repair continuation first runs the public cellular speed probe.
If it succeeds, stop. Otherwise read public network status, fix any observed
airplane/data switch, then handle the declared mechanism: enable carrier roaming
and toggle device roaming only if its observed setting was off; or inspect public
usage and buy one gigabyte only if usage reaches the observed total allowance.
The policy reads only actual public responses and the declared task mechanism;
it cannot inspect simulator state, labels, initialization flags or hidden causes.
Repeating a probe does not reveal additional hidden information in this static world.

Record two distinct outcomes: service immediately after the first attempted
command (or the current state for stopping), and service after the entire declared
continuation stops. Record actual calls and billed changes separately. Never call
a continued outcome an immediate consequence. Verifier probes are private,
read-only checks; their outputs are not actor observations or free actor actions.

## Verification, probabilities and costs

Require exact independent replay of all public responses, complete states,
expected errors, charges and labels. Check an independent stored-state cellular
predicate against a real public speed probe used privately by the verifier.
Preserve all unrelated fields and permit at most one correctly billed additional
gigabyte. Unlike the initial allowance repair fixture, a world with sufficient
allowance can succeed without purchasing data. Do not count Wi-Fi as cellular.
The device data switch is an allowed changed field in every family because every
menu explicitly offers it. Keep raw service availability separate from success,
which additionally requires frame preservation and correct charges. The utility
below rewards that verified success. Persist partial call journals on failure.

Average outcomes within identical visible histories using the frozen uniform
prior. Retain fractional probabilities. Do not choose the best action separately
inside hidden worlds before averaging. Value service at 20 simulated dollars,
charge 0.25 per attempted non-read command, and test inspection fees of 0.10, 2 and
10 per read call. Deduct actual additional billed dollars too. Prior customer
lookup is sunk cost; every later attempted actor call, including failures and
redundant reads, is charged. These are declared simulated utilities, not spending.

For observation value, compare the specified inspect/repair alternative's expected
return with the best offered blind/stop alternative under the same information.
This measures the value of that declared policy, not unrestricted optimal planning.
Audit that probabilities differ between immediate and continued questions, that
some identical histories have genuinely divergent outcomes, that redundant reads
carry cost, and that the price of evidence can reverse the preferred alternative.

This stage produces verified private branch receipts and an aggregate qualification
report. A separate question-admission stage must preserve exact pairings, context
limits, ownership and source provenance before any examples enter training. Three
mechanisms alone do not justify a new paid learning comparison.
