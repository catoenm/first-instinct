# Next local gate: real steps and terminal rewards for retail learning

This prospective stage qualifies the live reinforcement-learning interface over
the completed retail evidence worlds. Do not recollect the counterfactual corpus,
change its labels, query a model, rent hardware or train yet. Keep its entire
connected retail family in one training-candidate group and preserve the separate
telecom usage manifest. Simulator resets and validation rollouts are additional
runtime checks, not new independent tasks or training data consumption.

An episode loads a fresh copy of an admitted receipt's epoch-1 database into the
unchanged real retail environment. It starts with exactly that receipt's public
cached history, goal, prior and identifier bindings. A state restore must exactly
match its source hash. No initializer, condition identifier, verifier outcome or
hidden current field enters observations or action descriptions.

Offer individual public operations, not just complete prewritten repair sequences:
stop, read current user, read current order, and the two appropriate writes. For
address goals those writes are profile and order address changes; for payment they
are migration and cancellation. Build all arguments from the public cache and
requested destination/card. Freeze the complete offered menu, including its order;
it cannot change based on private state. Record menu coverage separately from any
future learned or dynamic proposer. No dynamic proposer is qualified by this stage.

`step` executes exactly the selected public tool, appends its actual response or
documented state-preserving error, and returns its negative attempt fee. While the
episode continues, it must not return goal success, current hidden truth, a free
verifier observation or a success-shaped reward. The selected stop action ends the
episode and pays 20 units only when the independent goal/frame/ledger verifier
passes. Pay the terminal reward exactly once. A maximum of six actor turns,
including stop, bounds each episode; reaching the limit terminates and verifies
once. Charge every executed tool attempt, including failures. Exclude already-paid
cache/background work. Do not convert refunded money into reward.

After termination, another `step` must fail without changing state or paying a
second reward. Invalid menu choices and unexpected infrastructure exceptions must
raise; the latter never become failed-task labels. A fresh reset cannot inherit
mutations or costs from another episode. Preserve partial journals, source hashes,
complete private states and public histories. Keep the existing socket/data-read
guards and exact expected-error handling.

Qualify three scripted controllers on every one of the 20 task/configuration
pairs: stop immediately; inspect public prerequisites then perform the goal's
appropriate write when useful; and choose the wrong-scope write (or cancellation
for the payment goal) then stop. The inspection controller must read only its own
public history. These are 60 primary runtime episodes and 60 independent replays,
**120 episodes maximum**. Assign the six predeclared read/write cost pairs in a
fixed, outcome-independent cycle over primary episode identities; replay the same
fees. Cap each episode at six actor turns and 30 seconds, the whole stage at 720
actor turns. Never retry an unexpected failure automatically.

Compare each controller's public tool sequence and terminal state with the
corresponding already-qualified fixed procedure for that exact initial world.
Check total reward against independently recomputed terminal utility minus actual
future actor fees. Require identical complete-state/public-response/reward replay,
all 20 worlds retained, all three goals and all six cost pairs covered, no hidden
success feedback before termination, and negative checks for double payment,
cross-episode state leakage, invalid actions and altered fee accounting.

The result must distinguish source world/goal counts, additional runtime episodes,
actual tool calls, actor turns including stop, and model training presentations.
No model or optimizer runs in qualification. Before a paid comparison, admit the
paired retail questions with exact displayed continuations, check token limits and
cross-source usage, and freeze the controlled three-arm learning recipe. Forecast
supervision alone, reward-only Proximal Policy Optimization and their combination
must start from identical declared weights, preserve general replay and retention
gates, and leave the reserved evaluation untouched until selection is fixed.
